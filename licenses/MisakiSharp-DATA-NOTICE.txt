Upstream modifications include selecting word lists, adding numbers/symbols
and uppercase variants, reserving held-out entries, probing pronunciations
through eSpeak-NG, filtering and sorting lookups, generating learned
letter-to-sound/context tables, converting to TSV, and gzip compression.
Frequency columns are not retained; selected vocabulary keys are retained.
Synthetic probes and other sources also contribute to the listed resources.

3. Chinese converted data

MisakiSharp.zh_g2p.tsv.gz and MisakiSharp.zh_g2p_v2.tsv.gz include converted
pypinyin/pypinyin-dict character and phrase entries, jieba dictionary entries,
Misaki overrides and phoneme mappings. large_pinyin merges phrase-pinyin-data
sources, including CC-CEDICT, Zdic and corrections. Exact input revisions and
the full merge history are not recorded in the generated tables.

The MIT notices for the wrappers do not replace the underlying data terms.
CC-CEDICT is credited to its contributors/editors, original creator Paul
Andrew Denisowski and former editor Erik Peterson. Its publisher currently
declares CC-BY-SA-4.0: https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/
Covered converted CC-CEDICT data retains its applicable ShareAlike terms.
The original publisher credit is preserved in MisakiSharp-COMPONENT-NOTICES.txt.
Unicode/Unihan's supplied copyright and permission notice is in that file too;
the current notice is an attribution snapshot, not an asserted input version.
Zdic's current terms section 4 declares CC0 1.0:
https://www.zdic.net/terms/ . Its declaration and CC0 text are retained in
MisakiSharp-COMPONENT-NOTICES.txt; CC0-1.0.txt also contains the full terms.

4. Other converted data and scope limitations

English gold/silver lexicons and ja_words derive from misaki (hexgrad,
Apache-2.0). Lexicon expansion, TSV export and compression are upstream
modifications. English tokenizer rules derive from spaCy; the small POS
perceptron learns its labels rather than shipping spaCy's model tensors.
Their original notices and conservative source-model credits are retained
in MisakiSharp-COMPONENT-NOTICES.txt. Exact model versions are unrecorded.
Japanese converted UniDic 3.1.0 data, including the compact connection matrix,
retains the BSD option and AUTHORS/COPYING files in UniDic-3.1.0-*.

The modified code replaces the reviewed number and punctuation source ports;
these data notices are not the basis for that code change. Ordinary output
from a GPL program is not automatically GPL. The bounded source check found
no concrete conflicting output term or identified protected eSpeak source
expression in these generated resources. The published project declarations
and scoped input notices support retaining their bytes. Residual dictionary
content uncertainty is documented, not a new permission prerequisite or a
blanket legal-clearance claim.
No permission or relicensing of third-party material absent from its supplied
grants is asserted here. No restrictive application terms override the
licensed extraction and reuse of the covered data. All twenty original
generated resource files are retained in the dependency-source asset.
