embedded resources. Actual jieba, pypinyin, pypinyin-dict, spaCy/model and

source reference declares MIT by external link, without a standalone text.

underlying source notices. CC-CEDICT attribution, Unicode/Unihan's current
notice, and Zdic's current CC0 declaration are included below. CC-CEDICT
is licensed under CC-BY-SA-4.0; the full legalcode is in CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt.
The conversion chain merges phrase data into large_pinyin and exports
pinyin/hanzi/phrase and phoneme rows to the MisakiSharp Chinese tables.
These converted tables differ from the original dictionaries. The exact
upstream data releases and the complete merge history remain unconfirmed.

version. See the data-source review for remaining dataset obligations.

source notices; no model weights were downloaded for this review.
